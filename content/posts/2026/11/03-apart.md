> This was my favorite read of the sprint! congrats! Also, good catch on fixing the baseline pricing bug in the underlying benchmark so high prices actually reflect collusion rather than a setup artifact.

> If you expand this into a full paper, I'd love to see what happens when the cartel forms organically rather than being pre-seeded. It's also worth digging into why the post-warning prices stay so sticky: IE, is it genuinely tacit coordination, or are the models just lazily anchoring on the numbers already sitting in the order book history?
